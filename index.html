Astrovoyage

Fly across the solar system, land on a planet and find out whether a human could survive there.

  1. Click a planet. See its temperature, gravity, atmosphere, water, radiation, what you would see, and whether life could exist.
  2. Land and walk. Use W A S D, Shift and Space. Then remove your protection to feel how fast the environment kills you.
  3. Take the Mission tab. Pack the right equipment within the weight limit and try to survive.

Best on desktop Chrome. Scientific values are approximate and vary by location and altitude.
